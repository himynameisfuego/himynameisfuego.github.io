Who I am

I’m an engineer, researcher, coach, player, and musician with a strong foundation in audio signal processing and a bias toward making ideas usable. I like projects that sit somewhere between research, software craftsmanship, performance, and creative experimentation.

What I work on

Most of my technical portfolio revolves around sound: decomposition, pitch processing, speech enhancement, and audio software. Alongside that, I build practical tools, explore machine learning, and create domain-specific projects that come from real interests and real needs.

What this site is for

This website is a cleaner front door to my work. It brings together my software projects, rugby background, and music in one place, with a dedicated page for each project and space to grow beyond a flat GitHub repo list.

Rugby

Player for 25+ years. Coach, competitor, and builder of rugby tools.

I have played rugby for more than 25 years and I currently play for Helsinki Rugby Club. With HRC, I have won 3 championships as a player and 2 championships as coach of the women’s team. I am also the current Head Coach of the Finland Women’s National Team.

Rugby is not separate from the rest of my work. It shapes how I think about leadership, systems, preparation, and applied problem-solving, and it has also inspired some of my software and analytics projects.
