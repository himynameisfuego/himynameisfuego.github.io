Rugby

Player. Coach. Video analyst.

There is no Leo without rugby. It shapes how I think about leadership, preparation, commitment, and applied problem-solving.

I started with Rugby Fiumicello when I was six, I did U18s with Rugby Calvisano, and then six years with Rugby Brescia before moving to Finland in 2019. I currently play for Helsinki Rugby Club: with HRC, I have won 3 championships as a player and 2 championships as coach of the women’s team. I am also the current Head Coach of the Finland Women’s National Team.

I currently hold a World Rugby Coaching L2 certification.
