About me

A few milestones tell the story best.

August 1994

Bursting onto the Earth scene

I was born in Gorizia, in the north-eastern part of Italy, but soon moved to Brescia where I spent my first quarter of a century.

January 2013
Studio

Playground Studio: my own home recording studio

Armed with a Behringer B-1, an M-Audio Fast Track, and FL Studio 10, I started recording and mixing a variety of local artists.

April 2016
B.Sc.

B.Sc. Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering

I completed my bachelor’s degree at the University of Brescia, building my engineering foundation.

October 2018
Aalto

First Aalto chapter

A four-month scholarship from the University of Brescia brought me to the Acoustics Lab at Aalto University as a research assistant and thesis worker, working on adaptive loudness compensation and audio equalization.

March 2019
M.Sc.

M.Sc. Communication Technologies and Multimedia

I finished my master’s degree at the University of Brescia with 110/110 cum laude, focusing my thesis on adaptive loudness compensation in audio reproduction.

April 2019

Research intern at bdSound

I also worked as a research intern in Assago, Italy, on noise reduction using adaptive filters and machine learning methods.

October 2019
Finland

Moving to Finland to start my Ph.D. at Aalto Acoustics Lab

I relocated to Finland and began the doctoral path that would anchor the next several years of research, teaching, and collaboration at Aalto.

January 2024
Varian

Research Scientist in Helsinki

Today I work in Finland as a research scientist while continuing to build at the intersection of audio, software, music, and applied problem-solving.

May 2024
D.Sc.

D.Sc. (Tech.) in Audio Signal Processing

At Aalto University, my doctoral work focused on transient processing for audio time stretching and peak reduction, under the supervision of Prof. Vesa Välimäki.
